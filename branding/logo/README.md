# The NickelOS logo

`nickelos-logo-flat.svg` is the NickelOS logo: Dresden's design (the blue
disc and the white N, 2026-10-29), cut from his logo sheet
(`../from-dresden/logo-sheet-flat-2026-10-29.svg`) by
`cut-dresden-logo-flat-svg.py`: his paths as they are, on a transparent
background. It's the one logo everywhere (Dresden, 2026-11-02): the
website and its favicons, Settings > About (Chrome 0110), setup's welcome
screen (0111) and the boot animation (`../boot-splash/`). It's a vector,
so every size is rendered from it.

`nickelos-logo.svg` is his version with waves in the disc, cut from
`../from-dresden/logo-sheet-2026-10-29.svg` by `cut-dresden-logo-svg.py`.
It was setup's welcome screen and the boot animation until 2026-11-02;
Dresden chose one consistent logo, so nothing uses it now.

`nickelos-logo-dresden.png` (241x245) is the earlier logo, cut from his
first mock-up (2026-10-05) by `cut-dresden-logo.py`; it's replaced by the
SVG wherever the logo appears.

Don't draw, trace or derive another logo or mark. One drawn here
(2026-10-11, `make-logo.py` and its SVGs) was removed on 2026-10-12.

The brush stroke (`../stroke/brush.py`) is not a logo: it's the website's
hero streak and the wallpapers' sweep, which Dresden likes and which stay.
