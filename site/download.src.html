What you need

  • A 64-bit Intel or AMD PC with UEFI firmware, and 4 GB of memory or more (8 GB is better).
  • A disk of 32 GB or more that NickelOS can have to itself: installing erases it.
  • A USB stick of 4 GB or more (16 GB or more to try NickelOS from the stick).

Installing NickelOS goes through it step by step: writing the stick with Rufus, Etcher, Ventoy or dd, Secure Boot, and the installer.

Secure Boot

With Secure Boot on, the first start from the stick asks you to enroll NickelOS's key once, in MokManager (how). The key is on the stick. To check it's ours, compare the fingerprint MokManager shows under View key 0 with this one:

Known issues

  • There's no recovery for a forgotten password yet. An administrator can remove that account in Settings > Accounts and make it again, but its files go with it.
  • NickelOS doesn't use the PC's TPM yet. Your home folder is still encrypted with your password.
  • Laptops with two GPUs (Intel or AMD with NVIDIA) haven't been tested yet.
  • NVIDIA cards older than the GeForce GTX 16 series run on nouveau, which is slow for games.

What works today is on the status board. To report a problem, see Getting help.

Updates

Once installed, NickelOS updates itself from its own server, signed with NickelOS's key, on the Stable channel. Updates has how it works.
